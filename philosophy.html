How I Think & Where I Come From

A personal look at my background in competitive problem-solving, strategic habits, and creative interests.

🚀

Odyssey of the Mind (States Level)

Back in 5th grade, competing in Odyssey of the Mind (OM) all the way to the state level shaped how I approach engineering today. It taught me hands-on creative problem-solving, working within strict rules, and thinking outside conventional boxes long before I started writing code.

♟

Chess & Strategic Patience

Holding a 1710 Rapid rating on Chess.com reflects my preference for strategic calculation and composure. Chess reinforces the habit of looking past immediate impulses, anticipating downstream consequences, and evaluating system state changes carefully.

📖

Deliberate Focus & Deep Reading

I intentionally step away from rapid short-form media feeds to protect my attention span. I prefer long-form books, sci-fi worldbuilding, and deliberate technical exploration. This deliberate focus gives me the patience required to trace complex bugs and build software with longevity.
