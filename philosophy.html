Mindset, Habits & Strategy

A look into how I process complex problems, make strategic decisions, and structure my day-to-day focus.

♟

Chess & Strategic Foresight

I maintain a 1710 Rapid rating on Chess.com. Chess has taught me to remain calm under pressure, avoid impulsive moves, and think several steps ahead. In engineering, this means anticipating edge cases, understanding trade-offs, and building systems that won't break down three steps later.

📖

Deep Reading over Quick Content

I make a conscious effort to avoid short-form video algorithms and mindless doom-scrolling. Instead, I spend my time reading books, studying technical docs, and learning deeply. It gives me the attentional capacity to sit with difficult engineering problems until I actually solve them.

⚙️

A Zero-Bloat Development Ethos

I believe modern software has gotten lazy. We run massive frameworks for simple tasks and waste user bandwidth because hardware has gotten fast enough to hide bad code. I strive to write clean, efficient, structure-aware software that does exactly what it needs to do with zero unnecessary fluff.
